# E-Business – Entrepreneur and Investor Platform

## Project Overview

E-Business is a university Final Year Project (FYP) developed to provide a platform where entrepreneurs can present their business ideas and connect with potential investors.

The main objective was to make it easier for entrepreneurs to showcase their ideas and for investors to discover business opportunities that match their interests.

The project consisted of an Android mobile application and a web-based platform, developed collaboratively by a team of three students.

## Project Objectives

- Provide entrepreneurs with a platform to present their business ideas and startup proposals.
- Allow investors to explore business ideas and review investment opportunities.
- Facilitate communication and potential connections between entrepreneurs and investors.
- Provide a centralized platform for discovering and presenting business opportunities.

## My Contribution

I was primarily responsible for the Android mobile application.

My work included developing the mobile application and implementing the entrepreneur and investor profiles. The application was intended to allow investors to explore business pitches and ideas and express their interest in potential investment opportunities.

The web application was developed by the other members of the project team.

## Technology Stack

The Android project uses the following technologies and libraries:

- **Android SDK** – Android application development.
- **Firebase Authentication** – Authentication integration.
- **Firebase Realtime Database** – Realtime database integration.
- **Cloud Firestore** – Cloud database integration.
- **Firebase Storage** – File storage integration.
- **Google Maps API** – Map integration.
- **GeoFire** – Location-based data support.
- **Glide** – Image loading.
- **RecyclerView and CardView** – Android user interface components.
